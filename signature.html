How to Set Up in Gmail

  1. Review the design options below and choose your favorite.
  2. Use the Light / Dark Preview toggles to verify contrast on both backgrounds.
  3. Click the green "Copy Signature" button. This copies the formatted rich text (including colors, links, and layout structures) directly.
  4. Open your Gmail, click the Settings Cog ⚙️ → See all settings.
  5. Scroll down to the Signature section and click Create new (or select your existing signature).
  6. Paste the copied signature directly into the box using Ctrl + V.
  7. Ensure the checkbox "Insert signature before quoted text..." is checked, then scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
