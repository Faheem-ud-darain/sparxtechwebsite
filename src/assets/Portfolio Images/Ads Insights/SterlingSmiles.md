# 📊 Recruitment Campaign Performance & Insights

### 🎯 Instant-Hire Recruitment Case Study (`2.png`)
* **Campaign Objective:** Recruitment & Lead Generation (Hiring Post)
* **Meta Leads Generated:** 73 Leads
* **Cost per Lead:** Rs 11.47
* **Total Budget Spent:** Rs 837.31 *(Calculated: 73 leads × Rs 11.47)*
* **Time to Hire:** Sourced, interviewed, and successfully hired a candidate within **24 hours (1 Day)**.
* **Top-of-Funnel Reach & Engagement:**
    * Unique Viewers (Reach): 2,178
    * Total Views (Impressions): 3,482
    * Post Engagements: 204
    * Link Clicks: 159
