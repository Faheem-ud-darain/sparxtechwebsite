## 💡 Key Insights for Animation & Design

### 1. Cost Efficiency vs. Volume Scale
* **The Narrative:** Campaign 2 was highly efficient, keeping the cost per lead incredibly low at **Rs 36.21**. However, Campaign 1 shows the power of scaling up: even though the cost per conversation doubled to **Rs 75.81**, it unlocked massive volume (579 conversations) and yielded exceptional financial returns.
* **Chart Idea:** A side-by-side animated bar chart comparing **Cost per Conversation** vs. **Total Leads Generated** to visually show the trade-off.

### 2. Massive Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
* **The Narrative:** In Campaign 1, spending roughly **Rs 44,000** generated over **Rs 300,000** in revenue. This represents a massive return on investment, proving the funnel's profitability at scale.
* **Chart Idea:** A split progress or donut chart showcasing **Ad Spend (Rs 44k)** vs. **Revenue Generated (Rs 300k+)** to highlight the dramatic disparity.

### 3. Top-of-Funnel Retargeting Potential
* **The Narrative:** Across both campaigns, video content dominated user activity, pulling in over **600K combined video views**. Campaign 1 has a high frequency score (~4.7), meaning unique viewers saw the ad multiple times, cementing brand awareness and driving action.
* **Chart Idea:** A funnel visualization or an animated wave chart transitioning from **Total Views** $\rightarrow$ **Video Plays** $\rightarrow$ **Link Clicks** $\rightarrow$ **Conversations Started**.
