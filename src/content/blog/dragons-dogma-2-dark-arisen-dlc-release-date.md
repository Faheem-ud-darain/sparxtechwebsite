## Innovative Gameplay Features & Additions

*   **12 Lost Rites Dungeons**: The expansion integrates 12 massive, newly designed subterranean dungeon challenges. These high-level gauntlets are scattered across both the new Norgan snowfields and hidden, previously inaccessible corners of the original base-game map.
*   **The Relic Appraisal System**: While exploring ancient, ruined forts buried under ice, players will unearth sealed, historical caskets. By returning these artifacts to specialized smithing merchants in capital outposts, players can initiate an "Appraisal," transforming raw artifacts into top-tier weapons, custom heavy armor sets, and powerful tactical scrolls.
*   **Chilling New Monster Lineup**: Players must completely rethink their tactical approach when facing off against towering Ice Giants capable of freezing your active Pawns solid with environmental frost breath, alongside emaciated Frost Dragons and spectral undead units.
*   **New Vocations**: The DLC introduces the **Eclipse Knight** (dark magic + greatsword) and the **Plague Weaver** (poison/bow hybrid) to provide players with all-new combat options.

## Pricing, Bundles, and Retail Availability

Capcom has confirmed a tiered consumer rollout designed to accommodate both veteran players and newcomers alike:

*   **Standalone DLC Expansion**: For players who already own the base game on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, or PC, the *Dark Arisen* content will be available as a digital download on **July 21, 2026** for **$29.99 USD**.
*   **The Complete Next-Gen Bundle**: For incoming Nintendo Switch 2 players or newcomers on existing consoles, a unified physical and digital package containing the base game and the *Dark Arisen* expansion will release on **October 9, 2026** at a premium MSRP of **$49.99 USD**.

To further streamline player acquisition ahead of the fall launch, Capcom announced that the standalone base version of *Dragon's Dogma 2* across PC and current home consoles will receive a permanent retail price drop starting June 24, 2026.

## FAQ: Dragon's Dogma 2 Dark Arisen

### Q: Do I need to finish the base game to play Dark Arisen?
A: No. The expansion is accessible from the main menu once you've completed the tutorial (approx. 1 hour). However, the recommended level for starting Norgan content is 50+.

### Q: Does my progress carry over?
A: Yes. Your existing Arisen and pawns load directly into the expansion. Any new gear found in the Labyrinth can be brought back to the base game.

### Q: Is there new game plus integration?
A: Yes. *Dark Arisen* content is fully available in NG+ with scaled enemy difficulties.
