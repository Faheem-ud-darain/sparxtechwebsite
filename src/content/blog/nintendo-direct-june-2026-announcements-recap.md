## High-Profile Game Breakdowns

### 1. The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake
Nintendo officially closed the show with a gorgeous reveal trailer for [Ocarina of Time Remake](/blog/ocarina-of-time-remake-nintendo-switch-2-trending). Built from the ground up natively and exclusively for Nintendo's next-generation platform, the title is targeting a late 2026 launch window. It transitions the classic art style into realistic graphics.

### 2. FF Resonance
A surprise crossover and JRPG console rebuild of *Final Fantasy: Brave Exvius*. Utilizing the acclaimed HD-2D engine, [FF Resonance](/blog/ff-resonance-final-fantasy-nintendo-switch-2) will release on October 22, 2026, offering tactical turn-based action.

### 3. Duskbloods
Legendary director Hidetaka Miyazaki has unveiled a gothic, 8-player PvPvE masterpiece coming exclusively to Nintendo Switch 2. [Duskbloods](/blog/duskbloods-fromsoftware-nintendo-switch-2) represents a major shift toward real-time tactical combat on next-gen handheld hardware.
