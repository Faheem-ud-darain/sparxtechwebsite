## What the Leaked Gameplay Shows

The leaked footage (about 45 seconds in total) appears to be recorded off a monitor – likely from a Rockstar QA tester or a press preview build. Key observations:

### 1. Vice City Map Interaction
- The player character is seen driving a modified **Cheetah Classic** down a neon‑lit street that strongly resembles **Ocean Drive, Miami**.
- Mini‑map reveals a much larger Vice City than in *Vice City* (2002), with distinct districts: *Port Gellhorn*, *Stockyard*, and a massive **Everglades‑style swamp** to the west.
- Dynamic weather: rain transitions to night fog in real time.

### 2. New UI Elements
- A redesigned weapon wheel now includes **dual‑wield** capability for pistols and SMGs.
- Health and armor bars are more compact, positioned above the mini‑map.
- A **“Trust” meter** appears when near a companion character – suggesting a relationship/faction system.

### 3. Combat & Physics
- Euphoria physics appear more advanced than *GTA V* – enemies react to shot placement with realistic stagger and grabbing.
- The player uses a new **takedown** move from behind (similar to *Red Dead Redemption 2*).
- Cover system is fluid; character can now “peak” without leaving cover.

### 4. Vehicle Handling
- Cars have more weight; drifting requires braking into corners.
- Motorcycles can perform **stoppies and wheelies** with dedicated button combinations.

## Loading Screen Leaks – Key Details & Easter Eggs

Three separate loading screen images were leaked alongside the gameplay. They seem to be placeholder screens (watermarked with “ROCKSTAR INTERNAL – NOT FINAL”).

### Leak #1: “Welcome to Leonida”
- Shows a **glossy travel poster** featuring a flamingo, a skyscraper with “VICE” lit up, and the tagline: *“Leonida – The Sunshine State of Crime”*.
- References: Leonida is the fictional state that replaces Florida, first teased in *GTA V*’s next‑gen upgrade.

### Leak #2: Character Selection UI
- A split‑screen showing two protagonists: **Jason** (appears to be a white male, tattoos, rugged look) and **Lucia** (Latina female, ponytail, athletic wear).
- Below each portrait: a percentage bar labeled **“Chemistry”** – indicating cooperative relationship stats.

### Leak #3: Online Mode Tease
- A silhouette of a private jet over a massive **casino resort**.
- Text at the bottom: *“Coming 2027 – GTA Online Evolved”*.
