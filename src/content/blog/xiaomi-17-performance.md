# Designing for Speed: The Xiaomi 17 Max Standard

The **Xiaomi 17 Max** is a beast of a phone, and it’s trending for a reason: it’s pushing mobile performance to its absolute limit. For web developers, this device represents a new "Performance Floor." If your site stutters on a 144Hz display like the one on the Xiaomi 17 Max, you’re losing the high-end consumer.

## The Importance of 120Hz+ Smoothness

On a standard 60Hz screen, minor frame drops are often invisible. On a high-refresh-rate flagship, every missed frame feels like a glitch. To achieve a "Xiaomi-smooth" experience, we focus on:

1.  **Hardware-Accelerated Transitions**: Using CSS `transform` and `opacity` instead of properties that trigger layout re-paints.
2.  **Lean JavaScript Execution**: Ensuring that heavy computations don't block the main thread.
3.  **Adaptive Refresh Rate Support**: Optimizing animations so they look great at both 60Hz and 144Hz.

### Performance as a Luxury Feature

In 2026, **Speed is a Brand Value**. A fast-loading, buttery-smooth website signals to your customer that your company is efficient, modern, and high-quality.
