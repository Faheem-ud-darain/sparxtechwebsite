# Tomodachi Life Update: Full Patch Notes (May 2026)

*Last updated: June 9, 2026*

Nintendo fans have been waiting for a significant refresh to the cult classic life simulator, and the **'Living the Dream' update** has finally arrived. This massive patch introduces features that players have requested for years, alongside stability improvements that make island life smoother than ever.

This commitment to Mii-centric gameplay is particularly interesting given Nintendo's broader next-gen plans. If you are excited to see your custom Mii characters in action beyond your virtual apartment, you will definitely want to look at the newly announced [Nintendo Switch Sports Resort](/blog/nintendo-switch-sports-resort-release-date) which fully integrates Miis on Wuhu Island.

---

## New Features & Content

### 1. Advanced Mii Personality Matrix
The core of Tomodachi Life—the Miis themselves—has received a major overhaul:
*   **Sub-Traits**: You can now assign secondary traits that influence how Miis interact during specific events (e.g., "Competitive" Miis are now more likely to start gaming tournaments).
*   **Dynamic Friendships**: Relationship levels now fluctuate based on shared activities, not just random RNG events.

### 2. Expanded Island Activities
*   **The Digital Hub**: A new building where Miis can "work" in tech roles, mirroring real-world career paths.
*   **Night Market**: Every weekend, a new market appears offering exclusive interior decors and rare food items.

## FAQ: Tomodachi Life 'Living the Dream' Update

### Q: Is this a paid DLC or a free update?
A: This is a completely free software update available to all players who own the game on Nintendo Switch. It will download automatically once connected to the internet.

### Q: Do Mii relationships reset after installing the update?
A: No. Your existing friendships, marriages, and children remain completely intact. The update simply changes how future relationship fluctuations are calculated.

### Q: How do I access the Night Market?
A: The Night Market opens automatically at the town square every Friday and Saturday from 8:00 PM to midnight local time.
