# The 2024 Apple iPad Lineup: A Comprehensive Guide

*Last updated: June 9, 2026*

Apple's recent refresh of the **iPad** lineup has brought significant power and design improvements across the board. From the ultra-thin iPad Pro to the versatile iPad Air, choosing the right model for your workflow requires a detailed look at the specs.

---

## 2024 iPad Specs: Pro vs. Air vs. Mini

| Feature | iPad Pro (M4) | iPad Air (M2) | iPad Mini (6th Gen) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Processor** | Apple M4 Chip | Apple M2 Chip | Apple A15 Bionic |
| **Display** | Tandem OLED (Ultra Retina XDR) | Liquid Retina (IPS) | Liquid Retina (IPS) |
| **Sizes** | 11-inch & 13-inch | 11-inch & 13-inch | 8.3-inch |
| **ProMotion (120Hz)**| Yes | No (60Hz) | No (60Hz) |
| **Best For** | Professional creators, power users | Students, office workers | Ultra-portable note-taking, gaming |

---

## iPad Pro 2024: The Uncompromised Powerhouse

Featuring the revolutionary M4 chip and a stunning Tandem OLED display, the new iPad Pro is a laptop replacement in every sense of the word. It's designed for creative professionals who need the absolute best in mobile performance, offering high brightness levels, double the contrast ratio, and deep blacks that make design work pop.

### Key Highlights:
*   **Tandem OLED Display**: Combines two OLED panels for outstanding brightness and HDR color accuracy.
*   **Ultra-Thin Profile**: The thinnest Apple product ever made, making the 13-inch model highly portable.
*   **Thunderbolt Connectivity**: Fast data transfers for external SSDs and displays.

---

## iPad Air 2024: The Versatile Sweet Spot

The iPad Air now comes in two sizes: 11-inch and 13-inch. Armed with the M2 chip, it offers incredible performance at a more accessible price point than the Pro. It is the perfect device for students, writers, and casual creators who want a large display without the premium Pro price.

### Key Highlights:
*   **M2 Performance**: Easily handles heavy multitasking, photo editing, and high-end gaming.
*   **Landscape Camera**: Front-facing camera is finally positioned on the landscape edge, making video calls look natural.

## FAQ: Apple iPad Buying Guide

### Q: Does the Apple Pencil Pro work with older iPads?
A: No. The Apple Pencil Pro features a new squeeze sensor and haptic engine that is only compatible with the 2024 iPad Pro (M4) and iPad Air (M2) models.

### Q: Can I use the iPad Pro as a full laptop replacement?
A: Hardware-wise, yes. With the M4 chip, Magic Keyboard, and a trackpad, it feels like a laptop. However, iPadOS limits some advanced desktop software workflows compared to macOS.

### Q: Is 128GB of storage enough for casual use?
A: Yes. For streaming, browsing, and casual note-taking, 128GB is plenty. If you edit 4K video or download large games, consider upgrading to 256GB or higher.
