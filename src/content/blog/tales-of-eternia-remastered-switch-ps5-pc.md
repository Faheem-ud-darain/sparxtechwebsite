## Why Tales of Eternia Matters to JRPG Fans

For JRPG enthusiasts who missed it during the original console generation, *Tales of Eternia* represents the absolute peak of 2D-era Tales combat. Key features include:

*   **Linear Motion Battle System (LMBS)**: Real-time 2D combat featuring aerial combos and tactical spacing, laying the foundation for modern action-RPGs.
*   **Dual Protagonists**: The story follows Reid Hershel and Farah Oersted as they attempt to prevent the collision of their twin worlds, Inferia and Celestia.
*   **Craymel System**: Collect and summon elemental spirits (Craymels) to unleash devastating battlefield spells and gain traversal abilities.
*   **Legendary OST**: One of composer Motoi Sakuraba's finest works, blending classical prog-rock with sweeping fantasy themes.
